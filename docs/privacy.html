Privacy Policy — OpenLP Cloud Sync plugin

Last updated: October 1, 2026

The OpenLP Cloud Sync plugin ("the app") syncs your OpenLP song library between your own computers using your Google Drive.

Data the app accesses

The app uses a single Google OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which permits it to view and manage only the Google Drive files it created itself (your song-library backup files) or that you opened with it. The app cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Google Drive.

Data the app stores

  • On your computer: your Google OAuth tokens (kept in the plugin's local data folder) and a local copy of your song database used to merge changes between machines.
  • In your Google Drive: the song-library backup files the app uploads, inside a folder the app creates.

What the developer does with your data

Nothing. There is no server, no analytics, no account system, and no telemetry. Your data never leaves your computers and your Google Drive. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the plugin removes its local data folder, including stored tokens. Backup files in your Google Drive remain yours; delete them from Drive at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/avandever/openlp-cloudsync.
